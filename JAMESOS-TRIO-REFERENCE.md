# JAMESOS trio and performance reference

All four drum lanes are muted. These are performance templates from the original design, not native three-track Clipper presets: Clipper synth mode is a single instrument with an optional second layer. Any adaptation must preserve the musical roles without pretending the hardware has three independent synth tracks.

## SYNTHWAVE TRIOS

### 1. Classic Driving Outrun (Punchy & Anthem-Ready)
- Track 1 (Low): [01] Outrun Sub | Oct 1–2, tight 8th-note pulse
- Track 2 (Mid): [03] Juno Brass 84 | Oct 3–4, sustained chords
- Track 3 (High): [07] Chrome Lead | Oct 4–5, melody + stereo delay

### 2. Shimmering Retro-Arp (Hypnotic & Textural)
- Track 1 (Low): [09] Kavinsky Growl | Oct 1, whole/half-note roots
- Track 2 (Mid): [05] Turbo Arp | Oct 3–4, 16th-note arpeggio
- Track 3 (High): [11] Retrowave Bell | Oct 5, sparse counter-melody + reverb

### 3. Dark Cyberpunk / Darksynth (Aggressive & Cinematic)
- Track 1 (Low): [13] Analog Tom-Bass | Oct 1–2, syncopated octave jumps (pseudo-kick)
- Track 2 (Mid): [15] Grid Runner | Oct 3, gated 16th-note ostinato, high resonance
- Track 3 (High): [14] Vangelis Swell | Oct 4–5, slow fifths/octaves + heavy reverb

### 4. Nostalgic Sunset Cruise (Warm & Melodic)
- Track 1 (Low): [02] Nightcall Pulse | Oct 1–2, steady PWM bass, LP cutoff ~35%
- Track 2 (Mid): [17] Midnight Skyline | Oct 3–4, off-beat plucks + dotted-8th delay
- Track 3 (High): [06] Laser Harp | Oct 4–5, expressive sync-style lead

## VAPORWAVE TRIOS

### 5. Late-Night Mallsoft (Lush & Warped)
- Track 1 (Low): [22] Muzak Slap Bass | Oct 1–2, syncopated 80s fretless groove
- Track 2 (Mid): [18] Mallsoft DX EP | Oct 3–4, slow detuned 7th/9th chords
- Track 3 (High): [19] VHS Choir | Oct 4–5, held top-line notes, panned opposite Trk 2

### 6. Hypnagogic Plunder-Loop (Dreamy & Ambient)
- Track 1 (Low): [20] Macintosh Pad | Oct 2–3, thick low-passed pad chords
- Track 2 (Mid): [21] Seapunk Pluck | Oct 4, off-beat stabs or broken arps
- Track 3 (High): [23] Lobby Sax | Oct 4–5, vibrato-heavy melody

### 7. Weather Channel / Smooth Jazz Fusion (Breezy & Polished)
- Track 1 (Low): [12] Arcade Octaver | Oct 1–2, LP cutoff lowered (~30%) for round bass
- Track 2 (Mid): [29] Late Night Weather | Oct 3–4, sustained sus4/11th chords
- Track 3 (High): [30] Poolside Marimba | Oct 4–5, syncopated tropical lead runs

### 8. Abandoned Atrium / Utopian Virtual (Spacious & Eerie)
- Track 1 (Low): [27] Plaza Organ | Oct 2–3, held root-fifth pedal tones (tucked back)
- Track 2 (Mid): [25] Crystal Fountain | Oct 4, sparse 8th-note arp + heavy stereo delay
- Track 3 (High): [34] Slowed Flute | Oct 4–5, long breathy notes + high reverb

## IDM & BRAINDANCE TRIOS

### 9. Clockwork Melodic Glitch (Crisp & Generative)
- Track 1 (Low): [43] Squarepusher Sub | Oct 1–2, tightly gated bass, low filter cutoff
- Track 2 (Mid): [35] Clockwork Modal | Oct 3–4, 16th-note melodic percussion + S&H LFO
- Track 3 (High): [39] Micro-Glitch Dust | Oct 5, sparse conditional trigs (30%–50% prob)

### 10. Nostalgic Tape Braindance (Warm & Fractured)
- Track 1 (Low): [50] Sub-Harmonic Pulse | Oct 1, deep slowly shifting sub notes
- Track 2 (Mid): [40] BoC Warped Tape | Oct 3–4, detuned triads with slow pitch drift
- Track 3 (High): [48] Fractured Chord | Oct 4–5, sparse stabs with S&H chord inversion jumps

### 11. Acid Drill & Bass (Hyper-Kinetic & Squelchy)
- Track 1 (Low): [37] Aphex Rubber | Oct 2–3, 16th-note acid line + S&H cutoff + accents
- Track 2 (Mid): [41] FM Metallic Ping | Oct 5, rapid FM hits (50%–70% prob) as hi-hat sub
- Track 3 (High): [45] Data Stream | Oct 4, 1–2 hits/bar (25% prob) + Punch FX stutters

### 12. Crystalline Ambient Glitch (Delicate & Generative)
- Track 1 (Low): [47] Acid 303-FM | Oct 1, LP cutoff ~20%, slow sustained sub notes
- Track 2 (Mid): [38] Glass Wire | Oct 3–4, polyrhythmic 5- or 7-step pattern + S&H Timbre
- Track 3 (High): [44] Inharmonic Bell | Oct 5, sparse bell tones + long release & delay/reverb

## QUICK PERFORMANCE & MIXING TIPS (NO DRUMS)

- Panning: Keep Track 1 (Bass/Sub) center; pan Track 2 & Track 3 20%–35% L/R.
- Polyrhythms: Set Track 1 = 16 steps, Track 2 = 12/15 steps, Track 3 = 7/9 steps.
- Live Drops: Mute Track 1 for 4 bars while Tracks 2 & 3 ring out, then unmute on 1.
